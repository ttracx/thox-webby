ThoxWebby inference runs in your browser via WebGPU. The browser still downloads application assets and model files, but prompts are not sent to an inference API. A usable WebGPU adapter is required.

  • Use Chrome 113+, Edge 113+, or Safari 18+ with hardware acceleration enabled.
  • Check chrome://gpu (or edge://gpu) and confirm WebGPU shows Hardware accelerated.
  • Some corporate policies or extensions disable WebGPU - try an unmanaged profile or disable the extension.
  • Headless or software-only runtimes often return no-adapter; run in a real desktop GPU session.

Kernels

What are Kernels?

Kernels are the low-level GPU programs that do the model's actual math — the matrix multiplications, attention, and normalization behind every token. And how well they're optimized can dramatically speed up inference.

  • WebGPU & WGSL. Each kernel is a WebGPU compute shader, written in WGSL — the language that runs general-purpose math on the GPU — entirely locally in your browser.
  • Agentic Kernel Optimization. Every kernel was generated by AI (originally by Fable 5, before it was shut down; preserved as-is in this THOX build), benchmarked on an Apple M4 Max, and refined through an evolutionary, genetic-style search toward the fastest version.
  • Blazingly Fast. ThoxWebby runs Gemma 4 E2B at up to ~250 tokens/sec on an M4-class GPU, pushing your device to its limits.
